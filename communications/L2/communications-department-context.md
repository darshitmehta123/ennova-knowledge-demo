# Communications Department Context

## Why this document exists

This is the AI-ready Markdown version of the Communications Department Layer 2 master context.

It describes how the Communications Department drives:
- pipeline growth,
- partner experience,
- founder visibility,
- brand trust,
- measurable KPIs,
- operating contracts, and
- Zoho-first traceability.

## 1. Vision, Mandate & Success Definition

### Vision

Make Ennoble's impact visible, consistent, and credible at national scale - to win trust, accelerate closures, and retain partners.

### Mandate

1. **Demand Gen & Nurture**  
   Move prospects from suspect -> lead -> meeting -> proposal -> closure.

2. **Partner Experience**  
   Convert projects into partner-delighting stories through films, data-backed updates, and PR.

3. **Founder & Brand Leadership**  
   Position the Founder and Ennoble as go-to voices in school transformation, STEM and CBE.

4. **Digital & Data**  
   Keep websites and channels measurable, compliant, and traceable in Zoho.

### Definition of success

- Marketing-sourced/assisted pipeline >= 30% of closures.
- >= 80% of inaugurations covered with PR.
- 100% of projects receive a visibility kit.
- Weekly Communications dashboard is actively consumed by Partnerships and CEO.

## 2. Annual Objectives & KPIs

### Growth & Pipeline

- Build a CSR funders universe and expand databases for HNI, family foundations, and foreign funders.
- Use email campaigns to generate 100 new meetings by July 2026.
- Track sent emails, open rate, response rate, and meetings.
- Nurture warmish leads using email drips, LinkedIn engagement, and performance marketing support.
- Identify the Top 25 CSR education spenders as priority cold-lead targets.

### Brand Assets & Institutional Storytelling

Maintain core trust assets:
- new website,
- corporate film,
- product concept notes,
- quarterly newsletter,
- annual report.

### Social & Community

- Grow followers/engagement organically by >= 15% across LinkedIn, Instagram and YouTube.
- Publish 3 unique posts per week per platform.
- LinkedIn operating discipline: 100 connection invites/day + 25 warmish-lead connects/day, within platform limits.
- Build 10 content buckets/year and 15 post ideas per bucket.
- Emphasize reels through an in-house editing studio.

### Partner Pride & Delight

- Create 10 unique engagement touchpoints until July 2026.
- Collect 20 appreciation letters/testimonials per year.

### AV & Visibility Speed

- Reduce film completion TAT to about 15 days after shoot.
- Set up an in-house editing studio.

### Events, Awards & PR

- Identify 15 CSR events/year: 10 Mumbai + 5 outside.
- Promote Founder/CB as speaker in 10 events.
- Pitch for at least 5 awards/year.
- Use networking to enable approximately 20 meetings/year.
- Run 1 partner retreat/year + 2-3 sales roundtables.
- Support partner milestones such as MoU, inauguration and completion with PR.

## 3. Org Structure, Roles & RACI

### Core responsibilities

- **Head - Communications & Marketing:** strategy, annual calendar, budget, agency oversight, dashboards, approvals.
- **Content & Story:** case notes, web/product copy, decks, press notes, editorial calendar, house style.
- **AV Lead:** film pipeline from script -> shoot -> edit, vendor panel, QC and delivery.
- **Insight Sales Pod:** data mining, list building, email drips, event RSVPs and roundtable operations.
- **Agency partners:** web/dev, design, PR and editing studio as needed.

### RACI examples

- Campaigns: R - Insight Pod & Content | A - Head Comms | C - Partnerships | I - CEO
- Website: R - Agency | A - Head Comms | C - Content/Partnerships | I - CEO
- Films: R - AV Lead | A - Head Comms | C - Execution site POCs | I - Partnerships & CEO
- PR/Media: R - PR & Events | A - Head Comms | C - Partner SPOC | I - CEO

## 4. Channel Strategy & Editorial Architecture

### Audiences

- CSR decision-makers: email -> meeting -> decks/films -> site visit -> closure -> PR.
- Plant/regional CSR teams: hyperlocal success stories, inaugurations and media.
- Government stakeholders: clean briefs, MoU stories and district coverage.
- Community/schools: human stories, maintenance culture and student showcases.

### Content types and platforms

Content includes:
- flagship before/after films,
- inauguration films,
- Journey of a School stories,
- outcome cards,
- founder POV,
- case notes,
- newsroom posts.

Primary platform: LinkedIn.  
Support platform: Instagram.  
Hosting: YouTube.

### House style

Tone:
- confident,
- empathetic,
- evidence-led,
- avoid hype,
- use facts responsibly.

Accessibility:
- captions on videos,
- readable design,
- alt text discipline.

## 7. Quality Gates & Approvals

Tiered review:
- L1 Creator
- L2 Function Lead
- L3 Head Communications
- L4 CEO only for partner-facing, legal/PR-sensitive, budget claims, or public numbers.

Universal QC includes:
- facts,
- partner approvals,
- names/logos,
- consent,
- captions/alt text,
- links/UTMs,
- file rights.

## 8. Compliance & Risk

- CSR claims must align to Schedule VII.
- Use only approved partner logos.
- Media releases must be cleared by the partner.
- Written consent is required for student/teacher photos and videos.
- Sensitive details must be redacted.
- Crisis communications use a single spokesperson, holding statements and a time-bound cooling protocol.

## 9. Vendor & Budget Management

Maintain vendor panels for:
- videographers,
- editing studio,
- PR retainer,
- web/dev.

Standard onboarding includes:
- SoW,
- rate cards,
- TAT SLAs,
- sample tests,
- IP/confidentiality.

No work without PO.

Score vendors on:
- TAT,
- QC pass percentage,
- partner feedback,
- reliability.

## 10. Dashboards & Reviews

- Weekly KPI rhythm: leads, MQL -> SQL, assets shipped, film backlog, PR wins, website health and social performance.
- Weekly joint review with Partnerships.
- Monthly performance deck to CEO.
- Quarterly brand audit + template refresh.

## 11. Cross-Team Interfaces

- **Partnerships:** provides ICP, targets, deal stages and objections; receives decks, one-pagers, films and meeting follow-ups.
- **Execution:** provides before/after photos, site contacts and dates; receives films, photo packs and inauguration PR.
- **Budgeting/Proposals:** provides BOQ visuals and cost transparency; receives proposal collateral visualization.
- **Programs (CBE/STEM):** provides utilization logs, teacher hours and student experiments; receives outcome stories and highlights.
