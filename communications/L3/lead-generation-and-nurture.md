# Lead Generation and Nurturing Workflow

> Demo note: This file is a structured L3 candidate created from the current Communications strategy source. It should be validated by the Communications Team Lead before production use.

## Purpose

Generate and nurture CSR prospects through databases, campaigns, events, social channels and direct outreach until relevant leads are ready for Partnerships engagement.

## 1. Lead Generation

### Inputs / Sources

Identify CSR databases and lead sources based on ICP through:
- external agencies,
- events,
- email campaigns,
- email openers,
- social media.

### Actions

1. Identify CSR databases aligned to the ICP.
2. Run email campaigns.
3. Share qualified or relevant leads with the appropriate follow-up owners.

### Priority and accountability

- Priority: P1
- Named accountability in the source: Sharadha

## 2. Lead Nurturing

### Build the insight function

Set up a Sales Insights Team that can:
- analyse suspects,
- identify prospective leads based on ICP,
- segment leads into warm and hot,
- mine relevant contact details.

### Direct marketing touchpoints

Identify and use relevant touchpoints such as:
- email,
- LinkedIn/InMail,
- roundtables,
- tele-calls,
- other relevant direct marketing channels.

### Email drip workflow

1. Identify email openers from the first campaign.
2. Create subject lines and follow-up campaigns.
3. Send personal emails to email openers and event participants.
4. Move interested people toward roundtable sign-up or sales interaction.

### Roundtable support

1. Prepare a roundtable event format for sign-ups.
2. Coordinate and confirm participants city-wise.
3. Prepare supporting assets such as:
   - pitch deck,
   - corporate profile,
   - product/project concept notes,
   - impact story videos.

## 3. Digital Nurture Support

- Fix identified corrections on the existing website.
- Develop the new website.
- Create awareness through LinkedIn and social media.

## 4. Definition of Done for This Demo Workflow

The workflow is complete when:
- relevant lead sources are identified,
- campaign/outreach has been executed,
- respondents/openers are classified for follow-up,
- contact details are available where required,
- the next conversion action is clear,
- the responsible person/team is identified.
